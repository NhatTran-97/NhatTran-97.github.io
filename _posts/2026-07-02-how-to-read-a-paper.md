My three-pass method for reading papers efficiently, adapted from S. Keshav. <!--more-->

## Pass 1 — The bird's-eye view (5–10 min)

Read the title, abstract, introduction, section headings and conclusion.
Decide: *is this paper worth reading further?*

## Pass 2 — The content (~1 hour)

Look carefully at figures and tables. Note the key claims and the evidence
for each. Mark unfamiliar references to read later.

## Pass 3 — Virtual re-implementation (several hours)

Try to reproduce the paper in your head (or in code). This is where you find
hidden assumptions and weaknesses.

## Template I keep for every paper

```markdown
**Problem:**
**Key idea:**
**Method:**
**Results:**
**Limitations:**
**What I can reuse:**
```
